# PromptDeck 대표 홍보 비주얼 생성 프롬프트

생성 방식: Codex 내장 `image_gen`

용도 분류: `ads-marketing`

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: PromptDeck website social sharing hero background, landscape
Primary request: Create a premium, editorial technology campaign visual that communicates turning a rough Korean presentation brief into a polished system of presentation slides, data diagrams, maps, posters, QR labels, and visual concepts.
Scene/backdrop: deep midnight navy workspace fading into warm off-white, with one luminous cobalt-to-violet workflow ribbon connecting several clean floating paper-and-screen compositions.
Subject: an organized creative production workspace; abstract presentation slide frames, diagram nodes, map contour accents, poster grid, QR-like geometric tile, and color palette swatches, all integrated as one believable professional design system.
Style/medium: high-end 3D editorial illustration mixed with precise flat interface geometry; restrained, modern, credible for Korean public-sector and business professionals.
Composition/framing: wide 1.91:1 composition, strongest visual cluster on the right two-thirds, generous quiet negative space on the left for later text overlay, clear hierarchy at thumbnail size.
Lighting/mood: focused studio light, confident, efficient, intelligent, calm.
Color palette: midnight navy, cobalt blue, electric violet, teal, warm ivory, small coral accent.
Constraints: no people, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no fake UI labels, no illegible glyphs. Keep the visual clean and professional, not futuristic sci-fi, not playful cartoon, not cluttered.
```

생성된 배경 위에 정확한 한국어 브랜드 문구를 HTML/CSS로 합성해 1200×630 PNG로 렌더링했다.
